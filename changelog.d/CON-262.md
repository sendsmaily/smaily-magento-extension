- A customer who logs in with marketing consent is now sent to Campaign Intelligence at that login, also when they never bought and are not in your contact sync, so that their visit and their store-created visitor token are linked to their account and they see their own recommendations after logging out. The customer goes first, then the login link, so a customer who has just registered is linked at once. A login link Campaign Intelligence answers with "customer not found" is sent again once, after the store has sent the customer; when the store does not send that customer (no consent, no order, not in your contact sync), the **Log** shows the row as failed with the reason, and **Retry** links the login once the customer has reached Campaign Intelligence. That also holds for login links that failed this way before the update.
