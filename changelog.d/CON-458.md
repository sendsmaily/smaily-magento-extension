- Each Campaign Intelligence automation under **Settings > Automations** now has its own **Test mode** switch, on until you turn it off. While it is on, **Send test email** sends one test email to the address you type — at first your own admin email address (**This address is added to the Smaily list as a test contact.**), and the card says in plain words why a test could not be sent. Turning **Test mode** off asks **Turn off test mode?** and then starts the email for your store's real customers at once — at first up to 10 emails a day, then 25 and 50 — and the card shows, for example, **Sending to real customers · up to 25 emails today**. Before, unticking **Test mode** and saving only asked Smaily to switch real sends on.
