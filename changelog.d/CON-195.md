- The abandoned-cart reminder now follows the website's contact sync mode, not whether contact sync is switched on, and the store checks the shopper's contact in Smaily just before each reminder goes out: a contact who unsubscribed in Smaily gets none, in every mode. Under **Subscribers only (consent)** and **Checkout opt-in only**, a reminder goes to a shopper who agreed to marketing emails in the store — a newsletter subscriber, or a shopper who ticked **Subscribe to our newsletter** at checkout for the cart, also without placing the order — or who is a subscribed contact in Smaily; every other cart is skipped, and its Log row reads **Skipped** with the reason. Under **All customers (legitimate interest)** every cart with an email address gets a reminder, except a shopper who unsubscribed from the store's newsletter and did not tick the checkout box for the cart. A subscription status now goes to Smaily only when the newsletter status changes in Magento — on the storefront, in the admin, through the API or by a Magento import — and a new subscription subscribes again a contact who unsubscribed in Smaily; the contacts import, a profile save without a status change, a guest order's email, the purchase marker and the automations leave the status in Smaily as it is. Under **All customers (legitimate interest)** a profile save or guest order of a shopper who unsubscribed in the store is no longer sent as unsubscribed: a shopper Smaily does not have yet is added as a subscriber.
