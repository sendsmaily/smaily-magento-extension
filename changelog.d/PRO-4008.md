- An abandoned-cart record that `bin/magento smaily:gdpr erase` marks as erased now stays while the shopper's cart is still open in the store, so no reminder goes to the erased address if that cart changes again later. Once the cart is ordered or closed, or Magento deletes it, the nightly tidy-up removes the record as before. Before, the tidy-up could remove the record after 30 days while the cart was still open.
