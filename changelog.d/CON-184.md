- A website that moves from **Per-language Smaily accounts** to **One account, per-language workflows** no longer starts the workflows picked for its per-language accounts. Before, an automation could still post such a workflow through the website's one Smaily account, which started the wrong workflow or was refused. Each of the two modes now keeps its own **Per-language workflows**: the Automations tab shows the workflows of the mode you pick, also when you switch cards before you save, and automations use only those. The workflows of the mode you leave stay saved and show again when you switch back; after a switch back to per-language accounts, check them on the Automations tab once the accounts are entered again, because a workflow the account does not list shows as **Workflow #… (not in your Smaily list — kept)**.
