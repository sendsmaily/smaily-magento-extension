- A website that uses another Smaily account than the one saved for all websites now always has its own automations, because the workflows saved for all websites come from that other account: its **Use its own automations on this website** stays ticked, and the initial setup's Automations step keeps them. The account decides, not where its login is saved: a website with its own login of the same account (the same subdomain and API username, as a 2.8.x upgrade often saved it on every website) keeps the automations saved for all websites, and so does every website while no Smaily account is saved for all websites. Saving another account for a website starts its automations with no workflow, so you pick them from its own account; unticking its own Smaily account and saving returns it to the automations saved for all websites. Such a website no longer starts a workflow saved for all websites through its own account — also where it had that account before this update — until you pick its workflows: **Settings > Automations** asks you to **Pick this website's workflows**, and the **Log** shows each automation as **Skipped**, naming the website. A store with one website is unchanged.
