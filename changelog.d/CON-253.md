- On a store with several websites, Campaign Intelligence now recommends each customer the products their own website sells, with that website's links. Each product is sent with the websites that sell it and its link on each of them, in each language of their store views; customers and orders are sent with their website; and the **Smaily recommendations** widget shows each shopper the products and links of the website they are on. Before, the link of each language came from whichever website's store view Magento listed first, so where two websites share a language a shopper could get another website's link, or a product their website does not sell. When **Share Customer Accounts** is **Per Website**, a shopper with an account on two websites is one customer in Campaign Intelligence, known by each website's account, so each website's widget finds them. Price and stock stay the same on every website. The products, customers and orders already sent get their websites only through new imports: once after upgrading, press **Run again** on the **Catalog**, **Customers** and **Orders** cards under **Marketing > Smaily Connect > Settings > Intelligence**. A store with one website needs no import: nothing changes for its shoppers.
