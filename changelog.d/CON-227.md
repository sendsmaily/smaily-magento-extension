- The **Customers** and **Orders** imports to Campaign Intelligence no longer stop at a row Magento cannot read. A customer whose store view no longer exists is sent without a language, and an order whose stored item options Magento cannot read is sent without them; any other row Magento cannot read is skipped, and the finished import says "… — N skipped because Magento could not read them". `var/log/smaily_connect.log` on the server names each of these rows by its id. An import that stopped with "The store that was requested wasn't found" or "Unable to unserialize value" completes now: press **Run again** on its card under **Settings > Intelligence**.
