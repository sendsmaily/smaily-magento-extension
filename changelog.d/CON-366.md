- **Edit design** now works in the window over the admin also in browsers that block third-party cookies, such as Safari, and also when your Magento admin runs on an address of its own in your store's domain (for example `admin.your-store.example`). If the design has changes you have not saved, closing the window first asks **Close without saving?**.
