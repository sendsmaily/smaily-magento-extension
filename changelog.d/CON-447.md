- A store whose display currency differs from its base currency now sends Campaign Intelligence every product price in the base currency, the currency its orders go in. Before, a product went in the display currency, so its price and the store's orders were in different currencies. The products already sent get their base-currency prices only through a catalog import: if your store's display currency differs from its base currency, press **Run again** on the **Catalog** card under **Settings > Intelligence**. A store that shows its base currency needs no import.
