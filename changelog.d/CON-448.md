- An order's or a customer's row that failed to reach Campaign Intelligence and is tried again later no longer puts the order as it was before (for example before its refund) or the customer's older details (such as an old email address) back after a newer row of that order or customer: the Log shows the older row as **Skipped** with the reason, and only the newer row is sent. The same holds for a row you press **Retry** or **Send again** on, and for two rows of one order or one customer that come due together.
