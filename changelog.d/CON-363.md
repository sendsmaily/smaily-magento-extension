- While Campaign Intelligence is not connected, a **Campaign Intelligence** banner replaces its introduction on initial setup step 4 and **Settings > Intelligence**, above the setup link field, and sits on top of the other Settings tabs and the Dashboard, where **Dismiss** hides it for 30 days. Its **Contact Smaily** opens Smaily's consultation page; when your Smaily package has no API access it says **Requires a paid Smaily plan** and offers **Log in to Smaily**. Once Smaily opens self-service starts, a store with one Smaily account sees **Try Intelligence free** instead: it creates your Campaign Intelligence store with the Smaily login the extension holds, in a window over the admin (or **Open in a new window**), connects the extension and starts the catalog, customers and orders imports. Once connected, **Settings > Intelligence** shows **Show recommendations in your store**, which names both ways to place the **Smaily recommendations** widget — a widget instance under **Content > Elements > Widgets** for product pages, the cart or the order success page, or the widget in a page or a block (in Page Builder, in a **Text** or an **HTML Code** element) — and links the guide.
