- A newsletter subscription that failed to reach Smaily and is tried again later no longer marks the contact subscribed after a newer change of that contact, such as an unsubscribe, already reached Smaily: the Log shows it as **Skipped** with the reason, and **Send again** and **Retry** refuse it.
