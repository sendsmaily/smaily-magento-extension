- Every automation now sends only a workflow of the Smaily account it goes through. Saving another Smaily account (another subdomain) in place of the saved one — also by switching to **Per-language Smaily accounts**, where the **Default fallback account** takes the saved account's place — removes the workflows picked from the replaced account, and the page asks first. Until you pick workflows of the new account under **Settings > Automations**, those automations send nothing and the **Log** shows them as **Skipped**. Before, they went on through the new account with the old account's workflow numbers, also after a switch back to one account. A website with its own automations now sends what its **Automations** tab shows: a workflow set to **-- Not Selected --** there no longer falls back to the one saved for all websites.
