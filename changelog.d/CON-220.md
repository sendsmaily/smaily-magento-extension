- Disconnecting Campaign Intelligence (`bin/magento smaily:engine:disconnect --force`) and uninstalling the extension now tell Campaign Intelligence that this connection ends, before the extension deletes its stored key (Campaign Intelligence API contract 1.18.0). If your store has no other connection, Campaign Intelligence pauses the store: it issues no recommendations and sends none of its automated emails, and it keeps your store's data. Reconnecting with a fresh setup link ends the pause; your store's data is deleted when you close your Smaily account. When Campaign Intelligence cannot be reached, the disconnect or the removal still goes ahead, and the command says so in one line.
