- **Settings > Connection** has an optional **Transactional emails** card for a separate Smaily account for order and shipment emails, saved for all websites (a website may use its own); once Smaily accepts it, **Settings > Automations** maps **Order confirmation** and **Shipment confirmation** to that account's workflows, per store language, each **Enabled** off by default. These settings send nothing yet — sending through Smaily follows in a later release, and Magento's own order and shipment emails go out as before.
