- A website with **Use its own Smaily account on this website** now starts the workflows picked under **Per-language workflows** through its own Smaily account. Before, they were posted through the Smaily account saved for all websites, which started the wrong workflow or was refused. The same holds for the workflows a 2.8.x upgrade carried over on a website with **Per-language Smaily accounts**: they now go through its **Default fallback account**. Saving the Automations tab of such a website also checks the saved workflows against its own account's list.
