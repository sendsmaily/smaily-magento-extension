- A website with **Use its own Smaily account on this website** now always has its own automations, because the workflows saved for all websites come from another Smaily account: its **Use its own automations on this website** stays ticked, and the initial setup's Automations step keeps them. Ticking its own Smaily account and saving starts its automations with no workflow, so you pick them from its own account; unticking it and saving returns the website to the automations saved for all websites. Such a website no longer starts a workflow saved for all websites through its own account — also where it had its own Smaily account before this update — until you pick its workflows: **Settings > Automations** asks you to **Pick this website's workflows**, and the **Log** shows each automation as **Skipped**, naming the website. A store with one website is unchanged.
