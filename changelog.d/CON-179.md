- The **Initial setup** sets Smaily Connect up once for the whole store, however many websites it has: it no longer asks which website you are setting up, it opens on its Connect step even when Smaily login details are saved already, and every value it saves applies to all websites. On a store with more than one website, the Connect, Contacts and Automations steps list each website that holds its own values (for example carried over from 2.8.x) under **Websites with their own values**; each one keeps them unless you pick **Use this step's values** for it, and the Contacts step names the websites its save starts contact sync on. On **Settings**, **Use its own Smaily account on this website**, **Use its own Contacts settings on this website** and **Use its own automations on this website** give the selected website its own values, and unticking one and saving returns the website to the settings for all websites. On a store with more than one website, **Per-language Smaily accounts** are set for one website, under **Settings > Connection**. A store already set up keeps every saved value and is not sent back into the setup.
