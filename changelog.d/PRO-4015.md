- Upgrading from 2.8.x: a website where 2.8.x had *Enable Subscribers Collection* on with no *Autoresponder ID* sent no welcome email, and its welcome automation stays off. An admin notice, **Smaily Connect upgrade: the welcome automation has no workflow**, now names those websites, so you can tick **Enabled** for **Welcome** and pick a **Smaily Workflow** on the initial setup's **Automations** step.
