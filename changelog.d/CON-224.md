- Deleting a subscriber in **Marketing > Communications > Newsletter Subscribers** (the grid's **Delete** action) no longer unsubscribes the contact in Smaily or turns their personalized recommendations off: a deletion is not an unsubscribe, and the contact may have reached Smaily from another source, so it keeps its status there, as in the WooCommerce plugin. To stop your emails to a subscriber, use the grid's **Unsubscribe** action instead; that still unsubscribes the contact in Smaily and, with Campaign Intelligence connected, turns personalization off. Deleting a customer account still changes nothing in Smaily.
