- Connecting Campaign Intelligence now starts the **Orders** import too, after the **Catalog** and **Customers** imports, so the order history no longer waits for you to start it. The orders go newest first: the last 18 months first, so that Campaign Intelligence can start, then older orders back to 60 months (Campaign Intelligence sets both numbers); no older order is sent. While it runs, the **Orders** card's bar shows the part being sent and says when the last 18 months are in. The **Customers** import now sends only the customer accounts your contact sync puts into Smaily and every customer with an order in that history. After upgrading, press **Run again** on the **Customers** card and then on the **Orders** card under **Settings > Intelligence**, so that your history is sent again in the new order.
