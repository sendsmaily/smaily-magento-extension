- **Settings > Connection** has an optional **Transactional emails** card: its **Send transactional emails** switch, off by default, shows a separate Smaily account for order and shipment emails, saved for all websites (a website may use its own); once Smaily accepts it, **Settings > Automations** maps **Order confirmation** and **Shipping confirmation** to that account's workflows, per store language, each **Enabled** off by default, with a **Customer details** switch, off by default, for the addresses, phone and order note. These settings send nothing yet — sending through Smaily follows in a later release, and Magento's own order and shipment emails go out as before.
