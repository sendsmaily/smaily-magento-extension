- On a new install, contacts start syncing to Smaily for a website only when its **Contacts** step of the initial setup (or **Settings > Contacts**) is saved, in the mode chosen there. Before, contact sync already ran once the **Connect** step saved the connection, in the default **Subscribers only (consent)** mode, before you had chosen how your customers gave permission; until the save, the checkout also shows no newsletter checkbox. The step now says "Contacts start syncing to Smaily when you save this step." A website that synced contacts before the update — from 2.8.x with its synchronization on, or from an earlier 3.0.0 release candidate with its Smaily account connected — keeps syncing without saving the step again.
