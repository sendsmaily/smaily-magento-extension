- Campaign Intelligence now gets fewer customers: a customer account is sent when it is saved only while the customer is in the scope the **Customers** import sends — a customer your contact sync puts into Smaily, a customer with an order in the order history Campaign Intelligence takes, or a customer who is logged in with marketing consent and saves their own account. Saving any other account (for example, under **Subscribers only**, an account that never subscribed and never bought) no longer sends it. A customer who joins later is sent at that moment: when they subscribe to your newsletter, or when they place an order. Customers Campaign Intelligence already has are not removed. After you change a website's sync mode, press **Run again** on the **Customers** card under **Settings > Intelligence** to send the customers the new mode adds.
