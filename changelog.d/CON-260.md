- A customer who logs in with marketing consent now sees their own recommendations in the **Smaily recommendations** widget also after logging out: when their browser holds no visitor token yet, the login sets one in the `smaily_rec_uid` cookie (365 days, set by your store's server), and logging out does not remove it. A token the browser already holds is kept. On a shared computer the next person on that browser sees those recommendations too, until the cookie expires or is deleted in the browser — add this to your cookie policy (the user guide's Cookies section and privacy-policy template have the text). Without marketing consent nothing changes. Linking a shopper's earlier browsing to their account at login now reaches Campaign Intelligence: until now it refused every such link, and each one showed as failed in the Log; **Retry** on a failed one now sends it.
