- On a store set up as **Separate stores**, the **Dashboard** now has the same website selector at the top as **Settings**, opens on your default website, and shows the website you pick: its Smaily connection and its own contacts and automations in **Contact syncs**, **Queued today**, **Failed** and **Recent activity** (Campaign Intelligence's figures stay those of the whole installation). Before, it showed only the default website's Smaily account and every website's deliveries together. Changing **Your websites** now carries the transactional-email account, its switches and its workflows as it carries the Smaily account, Contacts settings and automations: to **Separate stores**, every website keeps the transactional-email account it sends with as its own; back to **One store in several versions**, the selected website's becomes the one for all websites.
