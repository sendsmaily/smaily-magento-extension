- The abandoned-cart reminder now follows the website's contact sync mode. Under **Subscribers only (consent)** and **Checkout opt-in only**, a reminder goes to a shopper who agreed to marketing emails in the store — a newsletter subscriber, or a shopper who ticked **Subscribe to our newsletter** at checkout for the cart, also without placing the order — or who is already a subscribed contact in Smaily; every other cart is skipped, and its Log row reads **Skipped** with the reason. Under **All customers (legitimate interest)** every cart with an email address gets a reminder, except a shopper who unsubscribed from the store's newsletter. An unsubscribe made in Smaily is never undone by the store any more: the contacts import, an admin save of a customer or subscriber, and a subscription made through Magento's API leave it as it is; only the shopper's own new subscription in the store subscribes them again.
