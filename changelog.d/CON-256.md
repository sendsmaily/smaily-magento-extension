- An automation card on **Settings > Automations** and in the initial setup shows only its name, description and **Enabled** until **Enabled** is ticked; then it shows its **Smaily Workflow**, its own settings and, last, **Send a discount code**. The same goes for the transactional emails, the Campaign Intelligence automations and the **Per-language workflows** tables. Unticking **Enabled** keeps everything set on the card, and no discount code is made while an automation is off: an email of it that was already waiting to go out, or that is sent again from the Log, goes without a code. A Campaign Intelligence automation with a field to fix stays open while it is off.
