- An abandoned-cart reminder now finds the shopper's newsletter subscription also when the cart's address has a capital letter with an accent (`ÜLLE@example.test` is `ülle@example.test`). Before, a shopper who unsubscribed in the store could still get a reminder in the **All customers (legitimate interest)** mode, and in the other modes a store subscriber got it only as a subscribed contact in Smaily. An address that differs by an accent (`jäne@example.test`, `jane@example.test`) is never read as the other shopper's. `bin/magento smaily:gdpr export` and `bin/magento smaily:gdpr erase` likewise now find the discount codes of an address stored with an accented capital.
