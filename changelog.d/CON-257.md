- With Campaign Intelligence connected, every email card on **Settings > Automations** and in the initial setup — **Welcome**, **First order**, **Abandoned cart**, **Order confirmation**, **Shipping confirmation** and each Campaign Intelligence automation — shows **Edit design** while its **Enabled** is ticked, when Campaign Intelligence offers a design for that email, with the design's state and subject beside it. It opens the email's design page over the admin, where you edit the subject, sender and design and can send yourself a test email; **Open in a new tab** at its top opens it in a browser tab instead, and on an admin that runs on another address than the store it always opens in a tab. A saved design reaches Smaily only once Campaign Intelligence starts delivering designs to Smaily; until then your emails keep using the Smaily workflow you picked, with its own design.
