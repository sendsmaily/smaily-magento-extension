- A store with more than one language that has not saved a language card yet now has **One account, per-language automations** picked, as in WooCommerce, and saving stores it; until the save its automations work as before. A store that saved its language setting before keeps it. The three cards take the WooCommerce names: **Separate Smaily accounts** (until now **Per-language Smaily accounts**), **One account, per-language automations** (until now **One account, per-language workflows**) and **One account, one automation with branches** (until now **One workflow branching by language**).
