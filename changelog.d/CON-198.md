- Each automation — **Welcome**, **First order** and **Abandoned cart** — can now give every shopper their own single-use discount code: tick **Send a discount code**, pick one of your Magento cart price rules under **Cart price rule** (Coupon "Specific Coupon" with "Use Auto Generation") and set **Valid for (days)**, store-wide or as a website's own automations. The discount and its conditions stay in the rule; each email gets a new code that works once, until the end of its last day (never after the rule's To date), in the contact fields `welcome_coupon_code` / `welcome_coupon_expires_at`, `first_order_coupon_code` / `first_order_coupon_expires_at` and `abandoned_cart_coupon_code` / `abandoned_cart_coupon_expires_at` for your workflow's email. A rule that no longer gives codes sends the email without one, and the Log's **Details** say why.
