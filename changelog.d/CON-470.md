- A store with several websites run as one store on one Smaily account now shares its Smaily account, Contacts settings and automations across all its websites: **Settings** shows them once, for all websites, with no website selector, and a save there applies to every website; only the **Storefront URL** stays per website, with a field for each website. A store set up on 3.0.0-rc1 to rc12 whose settings sit on one website only gets them on every website with this upgrade, so the other websites' newsletter signups reach Smaily and start the welcome email from now on; to send the signups made there before, press **Run again** on the **Initial contact import** card under **Settings > Contacts**. Where a website keeps its own Smaily login of the same account or its own automations, the settings for all websites now apply to it. A website whose own contact settings sync differently (contact sync on or off, the sync mode, guest buyers, the checkout checkbox) keeps them until you confirm: **Settings > Contacts** lists such websites, and **Use the same contact settings on every website** applies the tab's settings to all of them. A contact that a website without a Smaily connection would have synced — a newsletter signup, a customer account, the checkout checkbox or a guest order — shows in the **Log** as **Skipped**, and the **Dashboard** names such websites, and says so when your websites use different Smaily accounts.
