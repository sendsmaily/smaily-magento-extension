- A store that sells in several currencies now sends Campaign Intelligence every order in its base currency, converted at the rate Magento stored with the order. Before, an order went in the currency the shopper paid in, and Campaign Intelligence added it up as the store's own currency, so the revenue it showed was wrong. The orders already sent get their base-currency amounts only through an orders import: if your store sells in more than one currency, press **Run again** on the **Orders** card under **Settings > Intelligence**. A store that sells in one currency needs no import.
