- Campaign Intelligence imports are much faster. Each cron run now sends to Campaign Intelligence until its queue is empty, instead of one batch of each kind a minute, and an import moves up to 2,000 products, customers or orders a minute: a store with about 80,000 orders and 20,000 customers has its history there in about an hour instead of more than a day. An import already running finishes much sooner once you update. New orders and other changes still go out within a minute or two while an import runs.
